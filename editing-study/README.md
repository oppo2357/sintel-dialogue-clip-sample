# Sintel · A 30-second editing study

[Watch or download the MP4](./sintel-30s.mp4)

A dragon fills the first shot. Two and a half seconds later, Sintel is alone in the snow. The audience gets the question before the dialogue explains why she is there.

| Time | What to look or listen for |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | A hard cut on the pulse carries the dragon's movement into the snow. Violet-blue shadows and restrained saturation make the snow feel colder than the memory that follows. |
| 0:05–0:11.25 | The score drops behind the dialogue. Subtitles sit below the photographed frame so both faces stay clear. Sintel's answer carries across the change of location. |
| 0:11.25 | A short dip through a dark blue-gray separates the conversation from the warmer memory. The picture comes up on the next downbeat. |
| 0:14.375–0:18.75 | Shortening the shots from three and one-eighth seconds to one and one-quarter seconds accelerates the action. The cut points land on a 96 BPM grid, exactly 15 frames per beat at 24 fps. |
| 0:22.5 | A 12-frame cross-dissolve blends the departing silhouette and dunes. The longer desert shot lets the music settle before the credit. |

The edit is 30.00 seconds at 24 fps with a 960 × 540 frame and stereo sound. The pulse, percussion, and tonal score were composed for this edit. The two spoken lines retain the actors’ original performances.
